Privacy Policy
{.display-4}

1. We {{< mark >}} do not {{< /mark >}} read, share or sell your data.
We are hosting Durare because we care about privacy on the internet.
It is maintained by volunteers and funded through donations from people like you.

1. Messages (stored on the server) will be deleted after 180 days.
To keep your history intact we recommend taking a backup of your chat history through your clients.

1. Our server is located in Finland and hosted with Hetzner based in Germany.
Please refer to Hetzner's Privacy Policy for their terms and conditions.

1. Applications like Gajim, Monocles and others support End to End Encryption (E2EE) using the OMEMO protocol. If E2EE is enabled, no one other than sender and recipients will be able to read the messages, not even the admins. You may have to manually enable encryption for each conversation in some applications.
We recommend enabling E2EE for all conversations.

1. Encryption without verifying whom you are encrypting your messages for is useless, to verify that their is no person in the middle, scanning QR codes or verifying keys through other means with the other party is recommended.
Please check on the xmpp client that you are using to see how to do this.

1. We provide and require SSL/TLS encryption on all provided services.

1. Federation - XMPP service provided by Durare is based on Federation Protocols. This enables users signed up at different service providers to interact with each other.
